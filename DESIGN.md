# tuhclip Design Direction

- Reading: compact transcript side panel for meeting participants, with a paper-document editorial identity.
- Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.
- Theme: fixed light theme reduces eye strain and preserves familiar document contrast during long transcript reading.
- Color: warm paper neutrals evoke physical notes; dark ink supports sustained reading; teal marks the active capture edge.
- Layout: one centered document surface keeps meeting status as the only decision on screen.
- Typography: system sans-serif stays crisp inside Chrome across platforms and at narrow panel widths.
- Spacing: a compact fluid scale preserves reading rhythm from 280px panels through wider side-panel layouts.
- Surface: one restrained shadow separates the active sheet from its paper stack without making every element float.
- Motif: the existing paperclip logo and offset sheet edge tie the extension shell to captured meeting notes.
- Motion: only hover and press feedback is used because persistent motion would compete with live transcript reading.
